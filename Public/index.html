Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is The Tech Guider AI?

The Tech Guider AI is an AI workspace for conversation, coding, research, creative work and projects through different Guider models.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes. Guest users can access the limited Guider Offline experience. Account-based features require authentication.

Which model should I use?

Guider Flash is designed for everyday tasks, Guider Pro for advanced general work, Guider Go for coding and projects, Guider Plus and Guider Plus Max for Content workflows, and Guider GPT for advanced, business-grade needs.

Is Guider GPT free?

No. Guider GPT is a paid model. Sign up and choose a plan that includes GPT access, such as Business, to unlock it.

Can I generate images?

Image generation is available where your account and selected model have the required entitlement and provider configuration.

Are my conversations saved?

Authenticated conversations can be stored through the platform's conversation system. Guest usage is intentionally more limited.
